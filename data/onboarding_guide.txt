New Hire Onboarding Guide — Novalink Devices

Week 1: Setup
Day 1 is spent in IT and HR orientation. New hires receive a laptop, an
employee ID badge, and access credentials for email, chat, and the internal
wiki. A welcome lunch with the immediate team is scheduled for Day 1
afternoon.

Week 1: Accounts and Access
By end of Week 1, new hires should have access to: the company email system,
the shared drive for their department, the project tracker (Jira), and the
internal wiki (Confluence). Access requests that are still pending after 5
business days should be escalated to the IT helpdesk.

Week 2: Role-Specific Training
Each department runs its own Week 2 curriculum. Engineering new hires shadow
a senior engineer on two code reviews and complete the "Local Dev Environment"
setup checklist. Sales new hires shadow two client calls and complete CRM
training.

30-60-90 Day Check-ins
- Day 30: Informal check-in with manager to discuss early wins and blockers.
- Day 60: Formal check-in covering progress against the role's initial goals,
  documented in the performance system.
- Day 90: Probationary period review. Successful completion unlocks benefits
  enrollment window extension and eligibility to apply for remote work
  arrangements.

Buddy Program
Every new hire is paired with a "buddy" from a different team for their first
90 days. The buddy is available for informal questions ("who do I ask about
X") and is not the same person as the new hire's manager.

Key Contacts
- IT Helpdesk: for laptop, account, and software access issues.
- HR: for benefits, payroll, and policy questions.
- Facilities: for badge access and desk assignment (on-site staff only).
