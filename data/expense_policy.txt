Travel and Expense Policy — Novalink Devices

Approval
Any business trip expected to cost more than $500 requires manager approval
before booking. Trips under $500 (e.g. a local client visit) can be booked
directly and expensed afterward.

Booking
Flights and hotels should be booked through the company travel portal
whenever possible. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours;
premium economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours.

Meals
Meal expenses while traveling are reimbursed up to $60/day, itemized where
possible. Alcohol is not reimbursed except for client entertainment, which
requires the client's name and company to be noted on the expense report.

Ground Transportation
Rideshare and taxi expenses are reimbursed for airport transfers and client
visits. Daily commuting to the employee's regular office is not reimbursable.

Submission Deadline
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being
incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days require VP-level approval and may
be denied.

Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, and upgrades to
business/first class (without prior approval) are not reimbursable.

Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expense reports are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, typically
within 10 business days of approval.
