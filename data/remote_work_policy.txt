Remote Work Policy — Novalink Devices

Effective Date: January 2026

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period and whose role
does not require daily on-site presence (e.g. lab equipment, hardware
assembly) may apply for a remote or hybrid work arrangement. Applications are
submitted through the HR portal and require manager approval.

2. Work Schedule
Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, 10:00 AM to
3:00 PM local time, regardless of time zone. Outside of core hours, employees
may arrange their schedule freely as long as weekly deliverables are met.

3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop, a monitor, and a one-time home-office stipend
of $400 for desk and chair setup. Employees are responsible for maintaining a
stable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload.

4. Communication Expectations
Remote employees must keep their status updated on the team chat tool and
attend the daily 15-minute stand-up call. Cameras are optional for stand-ups
but required for client-facing meetings.

5. In-Office Days
Hybrid employees are required to be in the office at least 2 days per week,
Tuesday and Thursday, for team collaboration sessions. Fully remote employees
(approved separately by department heads) are exempt from this requirement.

6. Review Period
Remote work arrangements are reviewed every 6 months. The arrangement may be
revoked if performance metrics fall below expectations for two consecutive
review cycles, with a 30-day notice period before the employee is required to
return to full on-site work.

7. International Remote Work
Employees wishing to work remotely from a country other than their country of
employment must get written approval from HR and Legal at least 60 days in
advance, due to tax and labor law implications. Unapproved international
remote work is treated as unauthorized absence.
